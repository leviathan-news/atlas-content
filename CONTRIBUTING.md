# Contributing to the Atlas mirror

Thank you for proposing a factual correction. A pull request is evidence for
editorial review, not a direct publishing command. Leviathan may verify,
decline, or independently apply an accepted correction through its audited
database publication path.

## Proposal boundary

For the initial pilot, change one existing territory and only one or both of:

- `atlas/<slug>/content.md`
- `atlas/<slug>/metadata.json`

Do not change the generated `atlas/<slug>/README.md` covers, `snapshot.json`,
`catalog.json`, schemas, policy files, licensing,
ownership, repository settings, or add/remove/rename a territory. Do not submit
raw HTML, scripts, symlinks, generated files, private correspondence,
credentials, allegations requiring confidential handling, or material you lack
authority to share.

Keep the proposal narrowly factual and cite primary sources. A protocol's
representative may correct a fact, but cannot unilaterally remove risk context,
rewrite history, alter a risk judgment, or turn an Atlas page into marketing
copy.

## Review and rights

PRs are public and attributable. Opening one does not grant a right to merge,
publish, or reuse the repository's editorial content. Until Leviathan publishes
formal inbound contribution terms, a PR is reviewed as a public proposal only;
accepted facts may be applied independently to the canonical Atlas record and
then mirrored back here.

By submitting a PR, you represent that its proposed text and sources may be
shared publicly for editorial review. Do not use a public PR for a legal,
security, privacy, or partner-sensitivity request; follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) where applicable.
